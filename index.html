ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Building strong foundations for a brighter tomorrow.

Located on the Kseri-Motla Road in Bolni, Dist. Rewari, Swami Dayanand Saraswati Sr. Sec. School has been a trusted name in education across Haryana. We provide a balanced environment of academics, discipline, and values — preparing students from Class I through XII for board exams and life beyond.

With experienced faculty, well-maintained classrooms, and a focus on every child's growth, we ensure no student is left behind.
